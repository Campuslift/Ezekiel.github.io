Our story

Why I built CampusLift TT

I built CampusLift TT because getting home from campus in Trinidad should not be a gamble.

Every evening the same thing happened. Class ended, I walked out to the road, and the wait began. Maxis came by already full. Route taxis slowed down, saw I was a student with bags, and kept going. Some days nothing came at all.

On a bad evening I stood there for close to two hours. On a normal one it was 45 minutes to an hour. Meanwhile cars drove past me with one person in them and three empty seats.

The struggle, in numbers

1h 45mmy longest wait for a ride
4+full maxis passed in one evening
TT$50+for a private taxi when I gave up
3 of 4seats empty in most cars on the road

It was not only me

I asked around. Classmates had the same story: leaving late lectures in the dark, paying for expensive hire cars, or walking to a junction hoping for a seat. Students with cars told me they drove home mostly empty and would happily share the fuel cost if they knew who needed a lift.

The problem was never a lack of cars. It was that nobody going the same way could find each other.

The idea

Match students

Connect people leaving the same campus at the same time and heading the same direction.

Split the fare

Share the cost so the ride is cheaper than a taxi and fair for the driver.

Keep it safe

Verify everyone with a school ID so you know who you are riding with.

Where it goes next

  • More campuses and partner schools across Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Recurring weekly routes for classmates on the same timetable.
  • Live trip sharing with a parent or friend.
