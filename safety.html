Safety

Getting home late should never mean getting in a car with a stranger.

Verified IDs

Riders and drivers sign in with a school and student ID, so everyone in the car is a student you can trace back to a school.

Share your trip

Send your driver’s name, car and plate to a friend or parent before you leave, and tell them when you arrive.

Report a problem

Rate every trip. Anything that felt wrong goes straight to us through the contact page.

Simple habits that help

  • Check the name, car and plate before you get in.
  • Wait for your ride inside the campus gate, not on the road.
  • Travel in pairs when you can on late evenings.
  • Sit where you can see the driver and the road.

Riders under 18 are matched only within their own school.
