Offer a ride

Driving to campus anyway? Your empty seats can cover your fuel and get a classmate home.

  • Set the fare yourself, up to the shared-cost cap, so nobody profits off students.
  • Only verified students from your school or a partner school can request a seat.
  • You approve every request. Say no any time.
  • Post a regular route once and it repeats each week.

1. Post your trip

Add where you leave from, where you are going, the time and how many seats are free.

2. Approve requests

See each rider’s name, school and rating before you accept.

3. Drive & split

The total fare is divided between everyone on board, you included.
