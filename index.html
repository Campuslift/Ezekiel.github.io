How to request a ride

  1. 1

    Sign in with your school ID

    Pick your school and enter your student ID so everyone on board is a real student.

  2. 2

    Enter your trip

    Set pickup and drop the pin where you are going. We work out the distance for you.

  3. 3

    Get matched

    A student driver heading your way accepts. You are told when they are close.

  4. 4

    Check before you hop in

    Confirm the driver’s name, car and plate, then ride.

  5. 5

    Split & rate

    The fare is shared equally. Leave a quick rating when you arrive.
